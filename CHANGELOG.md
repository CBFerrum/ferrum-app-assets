## 0.1.0

- First release: asset categories with per-company accounts, locations, assets with generated or
  manual depreciation schedules (straight line, written down value, double declining balance;
  pro-rata first period), depreciation posted by the periodic upkeep, capitalization from
  purchase invoices (with CWIP), movements, value adjustments, repairs, maintenance plans and
  logs, sale and scrapping, five reports, and the Asset Manager / Asset User roles.
